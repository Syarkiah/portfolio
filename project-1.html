Hafiz Indonesia 2023

TV Production

Project Overview

Hafiz Indonesia is a prestigious Quran recitation competition televised on RCTI. As a Production Assistant, I was responsible for coordinating various aspects of the production process for the 2023 season.

My Role

  • Managed day-to-day production logistics and coordination
  • Assisted with talent management and scheduling
  • Coordinated between production teams, directors, and technical crew
  • Ensured smooth execution of production plans

Project Challenges

One of the main challenges was coordinating multiple young participants and their families while maintaining a tight production schedule. This required exceptional organizational skills and the ability to communicate effectively with various stakeholders.

Results

The show was successfully aired on schedule, with positive feedback from both viewers and internal stakeholders. The production team received commendation for smooth execution despite the complex logistics involved.

Client: PT Rahawali Citra Televisi (RCTI) Duration: February - May 2023 Role: Production Assistant
